Ken Priore

I help companies build AI they can actually answer for. Most of my career has been in-house counsel, some of it as outside general counsel, always close to the builder and business.

I started in asset management, took a venture fund public on the New York Stock Exchange, was general counsel to a consumer platform, built privacy programs under GDPR and CCPA, and enabled the partnerships and IP behind enterprise software.

Every one of those jobs came down to the same thing: knowing what a system did, who allowed it, and who is on the hook.

Now that work has a name. In-house it is product and AI governance. Outside it, it is published research on proving what AI systems do and who authorized it.
